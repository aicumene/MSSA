# Companion preprint: From Oscillatory Modes to Source Geometry

**Alexandra Bernadotte, Ivan Menshikov.** *From Oscillatory Modes to Source Geometry: Identifiability and Localization in
Multichannel Time Series.* Aicumene Preprint 2026.058v1, November 11, 2026; SSRN working paper 7693202, https://ssrn.com/abstract=7693202, doi:10.2139/ssrn.7693202.
The companion of the MSSA preprint in `../paper/` (Aicumene Preprint 2026.044v2): that paper counts the oscillatory modes
of a recording and recovers their spatial loadings; this one turns the loadings into source positions.

| file | contents |
|---|---|
| `paper.pdf` | the preprint |
| `paper.tex`, `arxiv.sty`, `orcid.pdf`, `fig*.png` | LaTeX source; build with `pdflatex paper.tex` (three passes) |

The experiments of this paper are the localization scripts of `../paper/experiments/` (version 1) and `../paper/experiments/v2/`
(`v2_identifiability_checks.py`, `v2_robustness.py`, `fig_real_localization.py`, `fig_real_3d.py`, `v2_checks.py`).
